# Relic V2 — Librarian

The Librarian is Relic's persistent intelligence and collaborator.

It should feel like the same capable presence everywhere in the product without depending on one model provider.

## What the Librarian knows

Its working context is assembled from:

1. a small stable Soul defining identity, voice, truth laws, and tool behaviour;
2. concise World and Saga grounding;
3. the current authorized view, selection, and focus;
4. relevant recent conversation;
5. retrieved world facts and sources;
6. the task being performed.

The world itself remains the source of truth. The Librarian must not maintain a hidden parallel canon.

## What the Librarian can do

the vision is that the Librarian can do anything and has full access to all the tools and information in its workspace. in reality theLibrarian among other things is critical for a seamless environment and user actions such as:

- answer questions;
- search and retrieve;
- focus or navigate;
- arranging compositions/boards;
- create, edit, connect, or reorganize any material (in a penciled state);
- prepare sessions;
- propose changes to world understanding;
- use registered product actions.

The Librarian may **not independently establish Ink**.

Ink requires a deterministic, deliberate GM action outside model authority.

## Model strategy

The Librarian's identity, world grounding, truth rules, and action authority belong to Relic, not the model.

Models are replaceable workers behind a stable product intelligence.
