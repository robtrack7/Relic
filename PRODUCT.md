# Relic V2 — Product

Relic is a spatial worldbuilding and session workspace.

## The core pieces

### Desk
The default workspace: a fluid, pan-and-zoom canvas where the current world is arranged and explored.

### World Objects
Persistent identities in the fictional world. An object may begin as a loose Fragment and later deepen into a Character, Place, Faction, Thread, Event, Artifact, Note, or another useful representation without becoming a different thing.

### Cards
Visual representations of World Objects. Cards can be arranged differently in different contexts while still pointing to the same underlying identity.

### Boards
Saved compositions of the world. A Board stores references, layout, grouping, annotations, focus, and other view state. It does not own or duplicate world content.

### Focus
A deeper view of one or more selected objects without losing the surrounding world.

### Ink and Pencil
**Ink** is deliberately established world truth.  
**Pencil** is provisional, inferred, speculative, disputed, or unresolved material.

### Library and Search
Ways to find and pull existing world material into the current workspace. They are indexes into the world, not its primary home.

### Librarian
A persistent AI collaborator that understands the current world, Board, selection, conversation, and sources. It can answer, organize, create Pencil material, and operate Relic directly.

### Prep
A temporary working composition for the next session, built from the same world.

### Stage
The same world narrowed for live play: fast lookup, capture, deliberate assertions, Marks, and recovery without administrative friction.

### Since Last Session
A contextual view of what changed during play. New evidence and inference settle back into affected world objects as Ink or Pencil rather than moving through a separate content system.

## The core loop

**Capture → arrange → deepen → connect → prepare → play → settle → continue.**

Everything in Relic should make that loop faster, clearer, and more enjoyable.
