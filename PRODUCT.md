# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Game Masters building and running persistent fictional worlds. Between sessions they need reflective, flexible worldbuilding; during play they need immediate, trustworthy reference with minimal administrative friction, sometimes in dim rooms.

Worldbuilders and writers who never run sessions also use the same model. Players are a later, limited audience and see only deliberately published material.

## Product Purpose

Relic is a spatial workspace for creating, remembering, connecting, and advancing a fictional world over time. It keeps creative work from becoming document administration and helps the GM recover the right context quickly.

Success means capturing an idea without filing it first, finding world material during play, and carrying changes forward without losing narrative continuity.

## Positioning

**One persistent world, many contextual views.** Desk, Boards, Cards, Focus, Entity Builder, Prepare, Stage, Library, and Chronicle project the same World Objects rather than copying their content. The Canvas is a workspace view, not the world database. The Librarian is a contextual AI collaborator working within user-authorized operations, not a separate source of truth.

## Operating Context

Core loop: capture → arrange → deepen → connect → prepare → play → settle → continue.

Desk and Boards let users arrange World Objects spatially without changing their semantic relationships. Cards support recognition and exploration; Focus supports contextual inspection and rapid tabletop reference; Entity Builder supports deep, flexible authoring. Search and Library find objects independent of Board placement.

Prepare supports session planning; Stage supports fast reference and capture during live play; Settlement and Chronicle distinguish story changes over time. Threads connect evolving narrative concerns, Events, people, places, and developments; they are not simply task lists or audit logs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The canonical Product Laws, Glossary, and Component Catalogue govern implementation. Key distinctions:

- World Object identity persists across views. State varies by story position; story time is distinct from edit time.
- Pencil/Ink describe content maturity. Proposed/Pending/Settled describe authority and truth workflow. Player publication is separate from both.
- Spatial grouping and decorative connectors are not semantic Relationships.
- Fragment supports capture before classification. Evidence keeps provenance and is never treated as instructions.
- Librarian work is user-authorized and executed through validated semantic operations. Manual workflows remain useful without AI.
- V2 is the full web application; mobile/native adaptation comes later.

## Brand Commitments

Product name: Relic. Tagline: “Relic remembers your world so you can keep imagining it.” Banner: “Never lose the thread.”

## Product Principles

- **Think like a storyteller, not software.** Capture first; structure when useful.
- **One world, many views.** Never fork identity or silently turn Board layout into world truth.
- **Attention reveals depth.** Cards orient, Focus makes context usable, Builder supports sustained creation.
- **Truth and provenance remain explicit.** Show uncertainty, authorship, temporal position, and consequential changes clearly.
- **The GM remains in control.** The Librarian assists authorized work and is never required to use Relic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Interfaces need legible type, keyboard and pointer access, reduced-motion support, and fast tabletop scanning. Live-play surfaces must remain usable under low ambient light; the exact low-light visual treatment remains a design decision.
