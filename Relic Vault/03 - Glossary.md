# Relic Glossary

- **The Sanctum**: the literary, modern, relaxing surface for creation, organization, prep, review, approval, settings, and export.
- **The Stage**: the clean, focused live-session surface for agenda, pinned material, search, capture, recording, dice, and session closure.
- **The Loom**: GM-invoked, scope-aware conversational AI that reads, prepares registered typed actions, and never receives independent write authority.
- **Workspace**: ownership, billing, usage, and future-collaboration boundary.
- **World**: shared setting and World-canon boundary.
- **Saga**: a playable storyline inside a World; it owns active play and Session data.
- **Session**: a Saga's prep/run/review unit. Session prep is a Sanctum workflow, not a third product surface.
- **Canon**: approved truth. It is World-scoped or Saga-scoped and changes only through the authority path.
- **Draft**: a proposed change awaiting explicit GM review; it is not canon.
- **Source/evidence**: immutable or versioned material supporting a draft, answer, or review. Visibility is scope-checked.
- **Approval Queue**: the review surface for proposed canon changes, field diffs, conflicts, sources, and audit-producing commits.
- **Thread**: the continuity spine for unresolved story movement. The timeline is read-only in MVP.
- **Archived**: reversible removed-from-normal-use state. Hard delete requires the owning deterministic UI, archived/unreferenced state, exact name, and two-step confirmation.

Use **Saga**, not Campaign; **The Loom**, not Relic Guide; and **session prep**, not Studio or Workshop, in user-facing copy. `workshop_sessions`, `workshop_input`, `workshop_path`, `workshop_state`, `scaffold_saga`, `/guide`, `guide_*`, and `answer_saga_question` may remain internal compatibility names.
