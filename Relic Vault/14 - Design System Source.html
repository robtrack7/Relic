Design Principles
01
Editorial restraint
Every element earns its place. Whitespace is not empty — it is breathing room for the GM's imagination.
02
Warm, not cold
Parchment and stone over white and grey. Relic should feel like a well-made object, not a dashboard.
03
Literary hierarchy
Cormorant Garamond carries the world's names. Instrument Sans carries the UI. They never compete.
04
Amber as structure
Amber is geometric and consequential — used structurally, not decoratively. It marks canon and the things that matter.
05
The GM is the author
UI language is deferential. "Suggest," "draft," "propose." Never silent, never automatic.
06
The Stage strips back
Dark, fast, high-contrast. On The Stage, every decoration costs milliseconds. None are paid.
