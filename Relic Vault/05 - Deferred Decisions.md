# Deferred Decisions

These are not MVP requirements unless an active contract explicitly changes them.

- Rulebook RAG, mechanics extraction/adjudication, system binding, character sheets, initiative, encounters, tactical maps, VTT, and player controls.
- OCR/vision for scanned PDFs or images, image generation, broad corpus normalization, `.docx` extraction, and third-party archive integrations.
- Full relationship graphs/constellations, writable timeline editors, Era authoring, temporal contradiction detection, and World-canon promotion workflows.
- Multi-GM collaboration, player publishing/permissions, BYOK, local-model controls, and autonomous generation.
- Native Expo, Expo push, and deep-link parity; responsive web is the private-alpha client.

Text/Markdown and textual-PDF import, private static-image attachments, and GM-authored Stage references are MVP behavior; they must not be mistaken for rulebook RAG or visual AI.
