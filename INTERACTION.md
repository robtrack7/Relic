# Relic V2 — Interaction

The primary interaction model is a **spatial infinite canvas**.

## Desk and Boards

- Pan and zoom freely.
- Create or paste material without first choosing a type, title, folder, or template.
- Drag and arrange Cards directly.
- Multi-select, group, annotate, connect visually, and reorganize quickly.
- Save useful arrangements as Boards.
- The same World Object may appear on many Boards.
- Moving, grouping, annotating, or visually connecting Cards changes the composition only. It never silently changes world truth.

## Focus

Selection should deepen naturally rather than navigate away from the world.

Focus may reveal richer content, claims, relationships, history, provenance, sources, and editing controls while keeping the surrounding Board understandable.

## Librarian

The Librarian lives in a persistent, collapsible carriage rather than a separate chat destination.

The current Board, focus, and selection are implicit context.

When possible, Librarian actions happen in the natural medium of the answer:

- move or group Cards on the Board;
- create or edit Pencil material;
- connect objects;
- pull relevant material into Prep;
- focus or navigate;
- answer briefly with sources when language is the right medium.

Conversation records what happened; it is not the product itself.

## Responsive interaction

Desktop and tablet should exploit the spatial canvas.

Narrow screens should preserve **workflow parity**, not fake canvas parity: ordered stacks, focus, search, capture, selection, editing, Librarian actions, Stage, and review should remain fully usable without pointer-dependent spatial manipulation.

## Precedent direction

Use these as interaction references, not feature checklists:

- **Scrintal:** content-rich cards and spatial thought.
- **Heptabase:** one persistent knowledge network through many whiteboards.
- **Miro:** excellent canvas physics and direct manipulation.
- **Muse / Allume:** low-chrome, tactile, emergent organization.

Relic should synthesize these into something quieter, more atmospheric, and specific to fictional worlds.

**Target feeling:** a beautiful private desk where a living world has accumulated.
