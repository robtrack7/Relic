# Anti-patterns

## Dashboard gravity
Default home becomes stats, cards and CTAs. **Fix:** Saga opens to last Desk/Board state; status inhabits the world.

## Entity-page gravity
Every click navigates to a management page. **Fix:** progressive expansion and contextual right-side entity surface; canvas remains visible.

## Board-as-folder
“Move to Board”, one canonical location. **Fix:** Boards store placements/compositions only.

## Premature entity creation
New → choose type → fill form before thought exists. **Fix:** fragment first, structure later.

## Pencil = false / AI
Treating all GM content as Ink and AI as Pencil. **Fix:** Pencil/Ink is maturity; truth/provenance are separate.

## Current-row amnesia
Overwriting Mara's current values without retaining meaningful historical change. **Fix:** current projection + temporal story history.

## Audit = story timeline
Using database edit timestamps as fictional chronology. **Fix:** separate transaction time and story time.

## Snapshot explosion
Duplicating an entire Character for every Session or embedding every historical snapshot. **Fix:** one identity + temporal state/Events + as-of projection.

## Gantt Chronicle
Threads rendered as project-management lanes. **Fix:** temporal constellation/story currents with semantic junctions, clustering and semantic zoom.

## AI coordinates
Asking the LLM to invent x/y positions and splines. **Fix:** semantic operations + deterministic layout solver.

## Chat-only AI
Librarian describes changes rather than operating on the world. **Fix:** natural-medium semantic actions.

## AI-action bureaucracy
Every harmless rearrangement opens a confirmation modal. **Fix:** confirmation proportional to authority; reversible composition may happen directly.

## Review queue as world
Post-session continuity becomes 40 rows. **Fix:** Since-last-session delta in context + completeness index.

## Stage as mini-Desk
Tabs, inspectors, forms and full AI workflows during play. **Fix:** one live surface, smallest useful result, expand in place.

## Mobile canvas cosplay
Desktop infinite canvas squeezed to phone. **Fix:** focused/stacked default with optional full canvas mode.

## Hidden intelligence
AI silently changes settled truth or causes untraceable world drift. **Fix:** semantic operations are attributable/reversible; truth history remains inspectable.

## Fantasy SaaS
Ordinary CRM/wiki workflows with parchment styling. **Fix:** magic comes from persistent identity, temporal evolution, Focus, projections, Pencil/Ink maturity and Librarian agency.

## Feature islands
Timeline/map/relationships/session each own separate copies. **Fix:** shared identity + view-specific projections/working state.

## Over-modeling
Tracking every fact, belief, location and relationship continuously. **Fix:** temporalize what materially affects narrative understanding; unknown is valid.
