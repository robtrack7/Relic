# Relic V2 — Vision

Relic is a spatial workspace for creating, remembering, and running fictional worlds.

Its purpose is simple: **help a GM keep imagining instead of reconstructing continuity from notes, folders, and disconnected tools.**

Relic should feel like a living world spread across a private desk. Thoughts can begin loose and unresolved. People, places, factions, events, Threads, sources, and ideas become clearer as they matter. The same world is visible through the Desk, Boards, Focus, Prep, Stage, search, history, and the Librarian.

The AI is not a separate feature layered onto the product. The **Librarian** is the intelligence woven through it: aware of the current world and context, able to retrieve, suggest, organize, create provisional material, and directly operate the interface.

Relic is successful when a GM can return after weeks away, understand what matters, prepare and run the next session, and come back afterward to a world that has moved forward coherently.

## Product character

Relic should feel:

- spatial rather than folder-driven;
- atmospheric but restrained;
- powerful without enterprise-software density;
- immediate enough for loose thought;
- trustworthy enough for long-lived world continuity;
- intelligent without forcing the user into chat;
- authored, tactile, and quietly magical.

The goal is not to manage a campaign database.

**The goal is to make the fictional world feel present.**
