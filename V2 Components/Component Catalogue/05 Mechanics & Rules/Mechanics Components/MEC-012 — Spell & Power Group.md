# MEC-012 — Spell & Power Group

Parent: [[SYS-05 — Mechanics & Rules]]

## Purpose

Displays spells, powers, techniques, programs, magic, talents, or other structured abilities.

## Anatomy

May support group/level/category, name, Action Cost, roll/DC, resource cost, prepared/known/equipped state, concise description, Rules Reference.

## Boundaries

Template defines what spell/power means. Do not make D&D spell slots the universal model.

## Handoff

Build **MEC-012 — Spell & Power Group** as the generic template-driven structured-powers component.
