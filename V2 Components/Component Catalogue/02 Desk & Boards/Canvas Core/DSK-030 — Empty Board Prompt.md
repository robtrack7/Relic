# DSK-030 — Empty Board Prompt

Parent: [[DSK-001 — Desk Surface]]

## Purpose

Gives an empty Board just enough invitation to begin without turning the Desk into onboarding or a template gallery.

## Context & placement

Appears near the central visible Canvas only when the active Board has no meaningful placements/composition content.

Disappears as soon as the GM creates or places something.

## Anatomy

Three useful starting actions:
- Capture a Fragment;
- Paste or drop something;
- Ask the Librarian to help begin.

A small hint about double-click-to-capture may be included.

## Appearance & behaviour

Quiet, sparse, and integrated into the Canvas field.

No oversized empty-state illustration, checklist, progress tracker, or campaign wizard.

## Relationships

- [[DSK-010 — Blank Canvas Quick Capture]] — Fragment path.
- [[DSK-011 — Paste & Drop to Canvas]] — direct import/capture.
- [[LBR-001 — Librarian Carriage]] — assisted starting path.

## Handoff

Build **DSK-030 — Empty Board Prompt** as the minimal three-path invitation shown only on a genuinely empty Board.
