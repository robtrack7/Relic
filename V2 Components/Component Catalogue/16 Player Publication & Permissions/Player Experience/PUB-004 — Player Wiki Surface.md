# PUB-004 — Player Wiki Surface

Parent: [[SYS-16 — Player Publication & Permissions]]

## Purpose

Provides a read-only campaign compendium made from the player's authorized projection of the Saga.

## Product model

The Player Wiki is not a crippled copy of the GM workspace.

It is a simpler reading/browsing experience centered on the material the GM has intentionally revealed.

## Possible navigation

Initial information architecture can include:
- Home;
- People;
- Places;
- Factions;
- Artifacts;
- Chronicle;
- Sessions;
- Search.

Only include categories that have published material.

## Home

A useful player home can foreground:
- latest published recap;
- recently revealed/updated material;
- notable People/Places;
- known active Threads;
- recent published Chronicle entries.

## Excluded GM surfaces

Do not expose:
- Prepare;
- Stage controls;
- Settlement;
- AI proposal/change-set administration;
- GM Canvas editing;
- GM-only Sources/Notes.

## Read-only first

Player editing, comments, annotations and collaboration are deferred.

## Handoff

When built, design Player Wiki as a clean permission-filtered reading surface over existing identities/projections rather than another content database.
