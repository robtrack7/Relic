# FOC-BLK-001 — Identity Block

Parent: [[SYS-06 — Focus & Shared Focus Blocks]]

## Purpose

Provides deep identity presentation for a family-specific Focus.

It establishes what the focused thing is before the surface moves into narrative, mechanics, relationships, or history.

## Anatomy

Family-selected elements may include:
- primary name;
- aliases;
- primary image/emblem;
- type/subtype;
- role;
- title;
- pronouns;
- ancestry/species;
- category/scale;
- other identity-level metadata.

No family is required to use every field.

## Appearance & behaviour

Often visually blends into the top of Focus rather than appearing as a boxed section.

Name/image remain dominant.

Editable families can use Inline Object Rename and direct identity field editing.

## Relationships

- [[OBJ-004 — Object Identity Lockup]] — compact/shared identity composition.
- [[OBJ-006 — Object Image & Media Lead]] — primary image behavior.
- [[OBJ-009 — Historical & As-Of Indicator]] — historical context near identity.

## Handoff

Build **FOC-BLK-001 — Identity Block** as the flexible deep identity composition used selectively by family Focus surfaces.
