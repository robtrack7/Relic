# FOC-BLK-007 — Goals & Motives Block

Parent: [[SYS-06 — Focus & Shared Focus Blocks]]

## Purpose

Captures meaningful intent, desire, pressure, and conflict for Characters, Factions, Threads, and other goal-bearing World Objects.

## Anatomy

Potential structure:
- immediate want;
- long-term goal;
- fear/avoidance;
- pressure;
- contradiction;
- stakes.

Families select only useful dimensions.

## Appearance & behaviour

Narrative-first.

Avoid task-management checkboxes and project-management status metaphors.

Goals can be edited inline and deepened historically where the temporal model supports meaningful changes.

## Relationships

- [[FOC-011 — Focus Temporal Context]] — historical goal state.
- Thread/Character/Faction Focus — primary consumers.

## Handoff

Build **FOC-BLK-007 — Goals & Motives Block** as the narrative intent/pressure section rather than a task tracker.
