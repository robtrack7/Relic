# FOC-BLK-012 — Inventory & Holdings Block

Parent: [[SYS-06 — Focus & Shared Focus Blocks]]

## Purpose

Represents narratively or mechanically significant objects/assets possessed, controlled, carried, or held by the focused World Object.

## Use

Character:
- Artifacts;
- important equipment;
- keys/documents;
- meaningful possessions.

Faction:
- major assets;
- properties;
- resources.

Place:
- important contained Artifacts/holdings when family composition chooses to expose them.

## Boundaries

This is not automatically a complete RPG inventory or encumbrance system.

Families decide what counts as a meaningful holding.

Mechanics templates may separately contain system-specific equipment fields where required.

## Appearance & behaviour

Prefer rich World Object references and concise labels over spreadsheet-style inventory rows.

Open held objects directly into their own Focus/Peek.

## Relationships

- [[OBJ-ART — Artifact Representation Family]] — common held object.
- [[OBJ-010 — Inline Object Reference]] — compact reference.
- Mechanics system — may also contain mechanical equipment details.

## Handoff

Build **FOC-BLK-012 — Inventory & Holdings Block** as the campaign-significant holdings section, not a mandatory exhaustive inventory manager.
