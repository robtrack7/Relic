# OBJ-CHAR-03 — Expanded Character Card

Parent: [[OBJ-CHAR — Character Representation Family]]

## Purpose

Expanded in-place Character representation for a bounded amount of essential information while retaining spatial context. It is one surface, not a dossier or mechanics sheet.

## Context & placement

Occupies the same Placement identity and preserves its coordinates through disclosure transitions.

## Anatomy

- image and name;
- one short identity statement;
- at most three family-selected essentials (for Character, for example role, current place, or one relationship cue).

## Use

Useful during worldbuilding, preparation, Stage, and encounter reference when the Character should stay spatially present but reveal a little more context. It must remain bounded and must not grow into a compact dossier.

## Appearance & behaviour

Full detail remains in Focus and Builder. There is no user-facing flip, mechanics back, or third marker form.

## Handoff

Build **OBJ-CHAR-03 — Expanded Character Card** as the bounded richer Desk/Stage Character representation.
