# OBJ-CHAR-10 — Character Prepare Card

Parent: [[OBJ-CHAR — Character Representation Family]]

## Purpose

Richer planning view for the next Session.

## Anatomy

Identity, Session Relevance Cue, current state, immediate wants, relevant Threads, relevant relationships, selected meaningful knowledge, Character Mechanical Summary, GM prep note, pin/unpin.

## Behaviour

Prepare filters toward the active Session without rewriting durable Character summary. Librarian may suggest session-specific briefs/possibilities; those remain Session/prep material unless the GM establishes them as Character content.

## Handoff

Build **OBJ-CHAR-10 — Character Prepare Card** as the session-planning Character projection.
