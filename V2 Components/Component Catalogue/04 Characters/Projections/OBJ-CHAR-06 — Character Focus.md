# OBJ-CHAR-06 — Character Focus

Parent: [[OBJ-CHAR — Character Representation Family]]

## Purpose

Right-side Character Focus publication combining fictional identity, roleplay, temporal state, mechanics, relationships, knowledge, story history, references, and private GM material. It is the contextual exploration/editing layer, not a separate Character CRUD/detail page or the central Entity Builder.

## Context & placement

Opens in the right-side Focus carriage from the originating Character Placement, Library item, or reference while preserving Board, viewport, Placement, Stage/Prepare context, and Librarian context. During Stage, Stage Carriage remains left and Librarian remains available right when measured space permits.

## Focus pages

The page strip exposes only meaningful pages for the current Character:

- Overview;
- Mechanics;
- Relationships;
- History;
- Notes;
- Sources.

Sparse Characters omit empty pages. Page content is family-curated and may contain narrative, compact facts, mechanics groups, relationship sentences, evidence, or history without forcing one universal block anatomy.

## Major content regions

### Identity
Name, aliases, images, PC/NPC/adversary role, pronouns, ancestry/species, role/occupation and relevant identity metadata.

### Summary
Durable prose answer to **who is this person in the story?**

### Roleplay
Personality, voice, mannerisms, tells, beliefs/values, fears, bonds, flaws, secrets and portrayal notes.

### Goals & motives
Immediate wants, long-term goals, fears/avoidances, pressures and conflicting motivations.

### Mechanics
Rendered through [[MEC-003 — Mechanics Layout Renderer]] using the object's assigned full mechanics template/profile.

### Current state
[[OBJ-CHAR-19 — Character Current State Summary]].

### Knowledge & beliefs
Narratively meaningful Knows / Believes / Suspects / Was told entries, with story position/source where important.

### Relationships
Meaningful semantic relationships and their verbs/meaning.

### Threads
Current and historically important narrative Threads.

### History
Meaningful Events/state changes, not an audit log; historical selection can project the same Character as they existed then.

### Inventory / holdings
Narratively or mechanically important items rather than mandatory exhaustive inventory.

### GM material
Private secrets, improvisation, future ideas, hidden motives and reminders.

### Sources & rules references
Evidence for Character content plus mechanics/rules references.

### Builder
Character Focus exposes one clear **Open in Builder** action. Builder uses the same persistent identity and shared draft session for deep writing, block arrangement, and mechanics authoring without requiring a Board Placement.

### Librarian
Character Focus automatically becomes Librarian context when the GM invokes it.

## Mechanics customization

The overall Character Focus remains Relic-designed. The GM can edit the typed mechanics profile contextually and customize or replace the **mechanics block template** through the approved mechanics workflow, but does not freeform-rebuild the Focus page. Deeper block authoring belongs in Entity Builder.

## Appearance & behaviour

Deep editorial publication. Narrative, mechanical, relationship, provenance, and history material use different anatomy while remaining visually coherent. Wide layouts can retain the Desk and other carriages; narrow layouts stack pages and expose a compact page menu.

## Handoff

Build **OBJ-CHAR-06 — Character Focus** as the complete Character workspace composed from Relic narrative blocks plus modular game-system mechanics.
