# PREP-007 — Prep Checklist & Reminders

Parent: [[PREP-002 — Prepare Carriage]]

## Purpose

Provides practical Session-local preparation tasks and reminders that are genuinely utility/checklist content rather than narrative Threads.

## Examples

- Review Seraphine's abilities
- Print palace map
- Decide what Rhest knows
- Check grappling rule
- Charge recorder
- Bring handout

## Behaviour

Support add, complete, reopen, reorder, clear completed, carry forward selected items, and optionally surface selected reminders in Stage. Checklist state is Session-local utility state.

## Boundary

Checkboxes are appropriate here because these are actual preparation tasks. Do not apply the same task-management visual language to Thread.

## Relationships

- [[PREP-002 — Prepare Carriage]]
- [[PREP-010 — Stage Readiness & Session Packet]]
- Stage reminders

## Handoff

Build **PREP-007 — Prep Checklist & Reminders** as the lightweight practical task/reminder list for Session preparation.
