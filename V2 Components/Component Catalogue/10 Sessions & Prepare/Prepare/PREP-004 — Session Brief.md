# PREP-004 — Session Brief

Parent: [[PREP-002 — Prepare Carriage]]

## Purpose

Captures the high-level intent and opening conditions of the upcoming Session.

## Anatomy

- Session number/title;
- optional planned date;
- objective;
- opening;
- optional general GM note.

## Objective

Answers: **What is this Session fundamentally trying to accomplish, explore, or put under pressure?** This is GM guidance, not a completion requirement or player-facing quest objective.

## Opening

Answers: **Where and how do we begin?** Can include Inline Object References to Place, Character, Faction, Artifact, Thread, and Source/reference. Do not copy object summaries into the Session.

## Editing

Direct inline editing. Autosave. Sparse Sessions remain valid. No mandatory AI generation.

## Relationships

- [[PREP-002 — Prepare Carriage]]
- [[OBJ-010 — Inline Object Reference]]
- [[PREP-009 — Prep Librarian Context Bridge]]

## Handoff

Build **PREP-004 — Session Brief** as the compact directly editable title/date/objective/opening block at the top of Prepare Carriage.
