# REV-012 — Recap & Prep Implications

Parent: [[REV-002 — Session Settlement Workspace]]

## Purpose

Separates human-readable Session history and next-session suggestions from structured world mutation.

## Session recap

Relic produces a concise source-aware recap of what happened.

The GM can edit it.

Once accepted/settled, it becomes the official Session recap/history.

The recap itself does not automatically mutate every sentence into structured canon; structured Story Changes do that.

## Prep implications

Examples:
- Crown expects payment next Session;
- Rhest now knows the party lied;
- revisit missing courier;
- have palace map ready.

These are suggestions for Prepare, not story truth.

## Actions

Prep implications can:
- Add to next Prep;
- Dismiss;
- become a Thread/other Story Change when the GM decides it is persistent narrative state.

## Thread distinction

If the Session clearly established a persistent unresolved narrative Thread, create/update Thread as a Story Change.

If something is merely worth remembering next week, keep it as a Prep implication.
