# LBR-004 — Grounded Answer & Citation

Parent: [[SYS-13 — Librarian]]

## Purpose

Defines how the Librarian answers factual campaign/rules questions from authorized evidence while distinguishing retrieved truth from creative suggestion.

## Grounded answer

For factual questions, prefer settled structured state first, then authorized Sources/evidence, resolved against the relevant story position.

## Citation presentation

Use addressable lightweight citations such as `Mara Venn · Knowledge`, `Session 12 · The Bargain`, `Core Rules · p. 184`, or a transcript timestamp. Activation deep-opens the relevant target.

## Creative output

Creative ideation does not need fabricated citations. Generated possibilities must remain visually/semantically distinct from retrieved truth.

## Insufficient evidence

When Relic cannot establish an answer, say so and offer useful next actions rather than inventing facts.
