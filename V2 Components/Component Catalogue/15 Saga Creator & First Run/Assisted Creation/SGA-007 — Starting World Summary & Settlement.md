# SGA-007 — Starting World Summary & Settlement

Parent: [[SYS-15 — Saga Creator & First Run]]

## Purpose

Lets the GM recognize and accept an assisted starting world without a scaffold-review queue.

## Summary-first

The GM reviews the proposed Desk, obvious Relationships, concise Librarian summary and Session 1 seed. The mental question is: **Is this basically my Saga?**

## Primary action

In Supervised mode, `Use this starting world` settles the accepted [[SGA-006 — Starting World Change Set]] as one conceptual action. Removed/rejected proposals do not commit.

## Progressive detail

If something is wrong, select it on Canvas, inline edit, deep-open, inspect provenance, dismiss it, or ask Librarian to revise. No separate Scaffold Review page.

## Manual content

Content the GM directly creates manually follows ordinary authoring rules and does not need this assisted settlement boundary.
