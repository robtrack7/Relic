# SGA-004 — Build with the Librarian

Parent: [[SYS-15 — Saga Creator & First Run]]

## Purpose

Lets the GM begin from any fragment of an idea and use the ordinary Librarian to build a small playable starting world directly on the Desk.

## Entry

Open the normal [[LBR-001 — Librarian Carriage]] from the Desk. There is no creation-specific chatbot.

## Prompt

When invoked from an empty Desk, lightweight helper copy may ask: `What are you imagining?` and suggest that a premise, character, place, conflict, or half-formed idea is enough.

## Questioning

Ask only questions whose answers materially improve the starting Saga. No fixed interview. `Build from this` should always let the GM move forward once enough direction exists.

## Agentic workflow

`Build from this` is an explicit trigger under [[LBR-013 — Human-Initiated Agentic Work]]. The bounded task may interpret the brief, retrieve World-scoped canon, propose World Objects/Relationships, arrange a useful Desk, and seed Session 1.

## Direction

The GM can redirect the proposed world conversationally: remove NPCs, change tone, keep factions, make a Character central, or reduce scope. Changes remain proposals until settled.

## Scope

Generate the **smallest useful starting world**, not an entity quota. A Saga can begin with one Place, one Character, one Thread, and a Session hook.
